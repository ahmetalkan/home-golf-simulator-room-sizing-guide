Golf Simulator Ceiling Height and Driver Swing Clearance

Swing plane varies enormously from golfer to golfer: a 5-foot-10 player with a steep upright follow-through can clip a ceiling that a 6-foot-2 player with a flatter swing clears comfortably.

Practical clearance test before spending money

  • Stand on a stack matching your mat height: Quality shock-absorbing hitting strips sit 1.25 to 2 inches off concrete—simulate that height in golf shoes before testing.
  • Make slow full practice swings with your longest club: Watch the highest arc of both the backswing and the follow-through (the follow-through is often higher than the backswing).
  • Check overhead obstacles in garages: Standard ceiling-mounted garage door openers sit directly over the swing zone; converting to a wall-mounted jackshaft opener and high-lift door tracks reclaims valuable overhead clearance.
  • Irons-only compromise: If ceiling height sits around 8.5 feet, an irons-and-wedges practice bay still works cleanly when you leave the driver in the bag rather than developing a cramped indoor swing habit.
