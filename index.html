Home Golf Simulator Room Sizing Guide: Measure the Swing Before Buying Hardware

Indoor golf hardware only feels natural when you can make an uninhibited full swing without flinching at low beams, garage door tracks, or a close side wall. Measuring true usable room height, width, and depth—including the thickness of your hitting mat—prevents expensive hardware returns.

Modern launch monitors measure ball-flight physics and club-delivery metrics aligned with equipment and play concepts governed internationally by bodies such as The R&A. Indoors, however, high-speed golf balls also introduce impact and ricochet risks—making proper padded enclosures, safe eye-protection habits during setup testing (highlighted in MedlinePlus eye-injury and OSHA eye-protection resources), and clean projector/outlet wiring essential.
