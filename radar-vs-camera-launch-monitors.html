Radar vs Camera Launch Monitors: Space and Lighting Requirements

Room depth often picks your launch monitor category before brand preference enters the conversation.

TechnologyPlacementKey room requirement
Doppler radar unitOn the floor 6–10 feet behind the golf ballNeeds long ball-flight distance to the screen (often 16–20+ ft total room depth) and minimal interfering concrete-floor fans or fluorescent flicker
Side photometric cameraBeside the hitting mat facing the ballWorks in shorter rooms (~12–14 ft depth), yet must be moved sideways between right- and left-handed players
Overhead ceiling cameraMounted above and slightly forward of the hitting zoneClean floor with zero shifting for left/right play, but requires high ceilings (typically 9.5–10+ ft) for clear camera view
